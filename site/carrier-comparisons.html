How to use these pages

  1. Choose carriers that can support your phone and coverage requirements.
  2. Read the standard catalog rates and the amount required upfront for annual plans.
  3. Use Switch & Save to estimate your own 12-, 24-, or 36-month cost, including payoff and expenses.
  4. Check plan disclosures, taxes, hotspot and every promotional condition on the carrier website before ordering.
