How to choose a carrier

  1. Check which network is strongest at your home and work on the FCC National Broadband Map.
  2. Estimate how much data you use, then compare unlimited and low-data plans on that network.
  3. Confirm your phone is unlocked and compatible with the carrier’s IMEI checker.
  4. Follow our switching guide to keep your number.
