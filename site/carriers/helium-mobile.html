Frequently asked questions

What network does Helium Mobile use?

Helium Mobile plans in our catalog run on T-Mobile’s network. Check the carrier’s coverage map for your address before switching.

What is Helium Mobile’s cheapest plan?

The lowest-priced plan we track is Air 10GB at $15 a month. That is about $180 over 12 months before any intro deals.

Does Helium Mobile have unlimited data plans?

Yes. Infinity Unlimited (36GB high-speed) ($30/mo). Check each plan’s high-speed allowance and hotspot rules.

Does Helium Mobile offer family or multi-line discounts?

We haven’t found a published multi-line discount on the Helium Mobile plans we track, so each line costs the single-line price. Some plans are limited to one line.

Can I keep my phone number if I switch to Helium Mobile?

Yes. Federal number-portability rules let you keep your number. Keep your current service active, get your account number and transfer PIN from your current carrier, and enter them when you sign up with Helium Mobile.
