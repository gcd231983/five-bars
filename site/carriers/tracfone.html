Frequently asked questions

What network does Tracfone use?

Tracfone plans in our catalog run on Verizon’s network. Check the carrier’s coverage map for your address before switching.

What is Tracfone’s cheapest plan?

The lowest-priced plan we track is Freedom Unlimited at $45 a month with Auto-Refill. That is about $545 over 12 months before any intro deals.

Does Tracfone have unlimited data plans?

Yes. Freedom Unlimited ($45/mo). Check each plan’s high-speed allowance and hotspot rules.

Does Tracfone offer family or multi-line discounts?

We haven’t found a published multi-line discount on the Tracfone plans we track, so each line costs the single-line price. Some plans are limited to one line.

Does Tracfone have phone deals?

We track 1 phone offer from Tracfone. Most require a specific plan, and many require transferring your number. See the phone deals section for estimated first-year costs.

Can I keep my phone number if I switch to Tracfone?

Yes. Federal number-portability rules let you keep your number. Keep your current service active, get your account number and transfer PIN from your current carrier, and enter them when you sign up with Tracfone.
