Frequently asked questions

What network does Metro by T-Mobile use?

Metro by T-Mobile plans in our catalog run on T-Mobile’s network. Check the carrier’s coverage map for your address before switching.

What is Metro by T-Mobile’s cheapest plan?

The lowest-priced plan we track is Unlimited, bring your phone at $25 a month with AutoPay. That is about $305 over 12 months before any intro deals.

Does Metro by T-Mobile have unlimited data plans?

Yes. Unlimited, bring your phone ($25/mo) and Unlimited Premium 5G Data ($60/mo). Check each plan’s high-speed allowance and hotspot rules.

Does Metro by T-Mobile offer family or multi-line discounts?

We haven’t found a published multi-line discount on the Metro by T-Mobile plans we track, so each line costs the single-line price. Some plans are limited to one line.

Does Metro by T-Mobile have phone deals?

We track 4 phone offers from Metro by T-Mobile. Most require a specific plan, and many require transferring your number. See the phone deals section for estimated first-year costs.

Can I keep my phone number if I switch to Metro by T-Mobile?

Yes. Federal number-portability rules let you keep your number. Keep your current service active, get your account number and transfer PIN from your current carrier, and enter them when you sign up with Metro by T-Mobile.
