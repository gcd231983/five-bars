Frequently asked questions

What network does Tello use?

Tello plans in our catalog run on T-Mobile’s network. Check the carrier’s coverage map for your address before switching.

What is Tello’s cheapest plan?

The lowest-priced plan we track is Starter 2GB at $10 a month. That is about $120 over 12 months before any intro deals.

Does Tello have unlimited data plans?

Yes. Max Unlimited (50GB high-speed) ($25/mo). Check each plan’s high-speed allowance and hotspot rules.

Does Tello offer family or multi-line discounts?

Yes. For example, Everyday 10GB costs about $60 a month for four lines ($15 per line) at regular prices.

Can I keep my phone number if I switch to Tello?

Yes. Federal number-portability rules let you keep your number. Keep your current service active, get your account number and transfer PIN from your current carrier, and enter them when you sign up with Tello.
