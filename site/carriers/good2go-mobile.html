Is Good2Go Mobile a good fit?

Good for

  • Budget shoppers: plans from $5/mo.
  • Light users who mostly use Wi-Fi (low-data plans available).
  • Choosing the network that works best at your address.
  • Paying once a year for a lower monthly equivalent.

Watch out for

  • Annual plans are paid upfront and are usually non-refundable.
  • Some plans are limited to one line.
  • Some plans include no hotspot.
  • Some prices exclude taxes and fees.

Frequently asked questions

What network does Good2Go Mobile use?

Good2Go Mobile lets you choose among more than one major network when you sign up; the network available depends on your address and phone. Check the carrier’s coverage map for your address before switching.

What is Good2Go Mobile’s cheapest plan?

The lowest-priced plan we track is Annual 1GB (pay $60 upfront) at $60 a year (about $5/mo). That is about $60 over 12 months before any intro deals.

Does Good2Go Mobile have unlimited data plans?

Not among the plans we track. Good2Go Mobile focuses on plans with a fixed amount of data.

Does Good2Go Mobile offer family or multi-line discounts?

We haven’t found a published multi-line discount on the Good2Go Mobile plans we track, so each line costs the single-line price. Some plans are limited to one line.

Can I keep my phone number if I switch to Good2Go Mobile?

Yes. Federal number-portability rules let you keep your number. Keep your current service active, get your account number and transfer PIN from your current carrier, and enter them when you sign up with Good2Go Mobile.
