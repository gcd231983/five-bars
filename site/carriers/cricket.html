Is Cricket a good fit?

Good for

  • Heavy data users (unlimited plans available).
  • Paying once a year for a lower monthly equivalent.
  • Hotspot users who need a large allowance.
  • Getting a discounted phone when you switch.

Watch out for

  • Annual plans are paid upfront and are usually non-refundable.
  • Some plans are limited to one line.
  • Standard unlimited data can slow down when towers are busy.
  • Some plans include no hotspot.

Frequently asked questions

What network does Cricket use?

Cricket plans in our catalog run on AT&T’s network. Check the carrier’s coverage map for your address before switching.

What is Cricket’s cheapest plan?

The lowest-priced plan we track is 12-Month Unlimited BYOD — $300 prepaid at $300 a year (about $25/mo). That is about $300 over 12 months before any intro deals.

Does Cricket have unlimited data plans?

Yes. 12-Month Unlimited BYOD — $300 prepaid ($300/yr) and Supreme Unlimited ($60/mo). Check each plan’s high-speed allowance and hotspot rules.

Does Cricket offer family or multi-line discounts?

We haven’t found a published multi-line discount on the Cricket plans we track, so each line costs the single-line price. Some plans are limited to one line.

Does Cricket have phone deals?

We track 8 phone offers from Cricket. Most require a specific plan, and many require transferring your number. See the phone deals section for estimated first-year costs.

Can I keep my phone number if I switch to Cricket?

Yes. Federal number-portability rules let you keep your number. Keep your current service active, get your account number and transfer PIN from your current carrier, and enter them when you sign up with Cricket.
