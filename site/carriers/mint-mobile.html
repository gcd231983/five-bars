Is Mint Mobile a good fit?

Good for

  • Heavy data users (unlimited plans available).
  • Paying once a year for a lower monthly equivalent.
  • Getting a discounted phone when you switch.

Watch out for

  • Annual plans are paid upfront and are usually non-refundable.
  • Standard unlimited data can slow down when towers are busy.
  • Some prices exclude taxes and fees.

Frequently asked questions

What network does Mint Mobile use?

Mint Mobile plans in our catalog run on T-Mobile’s network. Check the carrier’s coverage map for your address before switching.

What is Mint Mobile’s cheapest plan?

The lowest-priced plan we track is Unlimited at $360 a year (about $30/mo). That is about $360 over 12 months before any intro deals.

Does Mint Mobile have unlimited data plans?

Yes. Unlimited ($360/yr). Check each plan’s high-speed allowance and hotspot rules.

Does Mint Mobile offer family or multi-line discounts?

We haven’t found a published multi-line discount on the Mint Mobile plans we track, so each line costs the single-line price. Some plans are limited to one line.

Does Mint Mobile have phone deals?

We track 2 phone offers from Mint Mobile. Most require a specific plan, and many require transferring your number. See the phone deals section for estimated first-year costs.

Can I keep my phone number if I switch to Mint Mobile?

Yes. Federal number-portability rules let you keep your number. Keep your current service active, get your account number and transfer PIN from your current carrier, and enter them when you sign up with Mint Mobile.
