Frequently asked questions

What network does Google Fi use?

Google Fi plans in our catalog run on T-Mobile’s network. Check the carrier’s coverage map for your address before switching.

What is Google Fi’s cheapest plan?

The lowest-priced plan we track is Unlimited Essentials at $35 a month. That is about $420 over 12 months before any intro deals.

Does Google Fi have unlimited data plans?

Yes. Unlimited Essentials ($35/mo). Check each plan’s high-speed allowance and hotspot rules.

Does Google Fi offer family or multi-line discounts?

Yes. For example, Unlimited Essentials costs about $90 a month for four lines ($22.50 per line) at regular prices.

Can I keep my phone number if I switch to Google Fi?

Yes. Federal number-portability rules let you keep your number. Keep your current service active, get your account number and transfer PIN from your current carrier, and enter them when you sign up with Google Fi.
