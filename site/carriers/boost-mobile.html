Frequently asked questions

What network does Boost Mobile use?

Boost Mobile plans in our catalog run on AT&T’s network. Check the carrier’s coverage map for your address before switching.

What is Boost Mobile’s cheapest plan?

The lowest-priced plan we track is Unlimited at $25 a month. That is about $300 over 12 months before any intro deals.

Does Boost Mobile have unlimited data plans?

Yes. Unlimited ($25/mo). Check each plan’s high-speed allowance and hotspot rules.

Does Boost Mobile offer family or multi-line discounts?

We haven’t found a published multi-line discount on the Boost Mobile plans we track, so each line costs the single-line price. Some plans are limited to one line.

Does Boost Mobile have phone deals?

We track 7 phone offers from Boost Mobile. Most require a specific plan, and many require transferring your number. See the phone deals section for estimated first-year costs.

Can I keep my phone number if I switch to Boost Mobile?

Yes. Federal number-portability rules let you keep your number. Keep your current service active, get your account number and transfer PIN from your current carrier, and enter them when you sign up with Boost Mobile.
