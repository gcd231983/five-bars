Frequently asked questions

What network does Total Wireless use?

Total Wireless plans in our catalog run on Verizon’s network. Check the carrier’s coverage map for your address before switching.

What is Total Wireless’s cheapest plan?

The lowest-priced plan we track is Total MAX 5G, bring your phone at $20 a month with AutoPay. That is about $250 over 12 months before any intro deals.

Does Total Wireless have unlimited data plans?

Yes. Total MAX 5G, bring your phone ($20/mo) and Total ALL ACCESS ($60/mo). Check each plan’s high-speed allowance and hotspot rules.

Does Total Wireless offer family or multi-line discounts?

We haven’t found a published multi-line discount on the Total Wireless plans we track, so each line costs the single-line price. Some plans are limited to one line.

Does Total Wireless have phone deals?

We track 5 phone offers from Total Wireless. Most require a specific plan, and many require transferring your number. See the phone deals section for estimated first-year costs.

Can I keep my phone number if I switch to Total Wireless?

Yes. Federal number-portability rules let you keep your number. Keep your current service active, get your account number and transfer PIN from your current carrier, and enter them when you sign up with Total Wireless.
