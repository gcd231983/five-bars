Frequently asked questions

What network does Red Pocket Mobile use?

Red Pocket Mobile lets you choose among more than one major network when you sign up; the network available depends on your address and phone. Check the carrier’s coverage map for your address before switching.

What is Red Pocket Mobile’s cheapest plan?

The lowest-priced plan we track is Essentials 3GB at $10 a month. That is about $120 over 12 months before any intro deals.

Does Red Pocket Mobile have unlimited data plans?

Yes. Premium Unlimited (50GB high-speed) ($30/mo). Check each plan’s high-speed allowance and hotspot rules.

Does Red Pocket Mobile offer family or multi-line discounts?

We haven’t found a published multi-line discount on the Red Pocket Mobile plans we track, so each line costs the single-line price. Some plans are limited to one line.

Can I keep my phone number if I switch to Red Pocket Mobile?

Yes. Federal number-portability rules let you keep your number. Keep your current service active, get your account number and transfer PIN from your current carrier, and enter them when you sign up with Red Pocket Mobile.
