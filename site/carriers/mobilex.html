Frequently asked questions

What network does MobileX use?

MobileX plans in our catalog run on Verizon’s network. Check the carrier’s coverage map for your address before switching.

What is MobileX’s cheapest plan?

The lowest-priced plan we track is Unlimited 10 (10GB high-speed) at $14.88 a month. That is about $178.56 over 12 months before any intro deals.

Does MobileX have unlimited data plans?

Yes. Unlimited 10 (10GB high-speed) ($14.88/mo) and Unlimited 30 (30GB high-speed) ($24.88/mo). Check each plan’s high-speed allowance and hotspot rules.

Does MobileX offer family or multi-line discounts?

We haven’t found a published multi-line discount on the MobileX plans we track, so each line costs the single-line price. Some plans are limited to one line.

Can I keep my phone number if I switch to MobileX?

Yes. Federal number-portability rules let you keep your number. Keep your current service active, get your account number and transfer PIN from your current carrier, and enter them when you sign up with MobileX.
