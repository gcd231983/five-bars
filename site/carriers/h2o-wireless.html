Frequently asked questions

What network does H2O Wireless use?

H2O Wireless plans in our catalog run on AT&T’s network. Check the carrier’s coverage map for your address before switching.

What is H2O Wireless’s cheapest plan?

The lowest-priced plan we track is Annual 10GB (pay $192 upfront) at $192 a year (about $16/mo). That is about $192 over 12 months before any intro deals.

Does H2O Wireless have unlimited data plans?

Yes. Annual Unlimited (30GB high-speed; $288 upfront) ($288/yr) and Unlimited (30GB high-speed) ($30/mo). Check each plan’s high-speed allowance and hotspot rules.

Does H2O Wireless offer family or multi-line discounts?

We haven’t found a published multi-line discount on the H2O Wireless plans we track, so each line costs the single-line price. Some plans are limited to one line.

Can I keep my phone number if I switch to H2O Wireless?

Yes. Federal number-portability rules let you keep your number. Keep your current service active, get your account number and transfer PIN from your current carrier, and enter them when you sign up with H2O Wireless.
