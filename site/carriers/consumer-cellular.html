Frequently asked questions

What network does Consumer Cellular use?

Consumer Cellular plans in our catalog run on AT&T’s network. Check the carrier’s coverage map for your address before switching.

What is Consumer Cellular’s cheapest plan?

The lowest-priced plan we track is Basic 1GB at $20 a month with AutoPay and paperless billing. That is about $310 over 12 months before any intro deals.

Does Consumer Cellular have unlimited data plans?

Not among the plans we track. Consumer Cellular focuses on plans with a fixed amount of data.

Does Consumer Cellular offer family or multi-line discounts?

We haven’t found a published multi-line discount on the Consumer Cellular plans we track, so each line costs the single-line price. Some plans are limited to one line.

Can I keep my phone number if I switch to Consumer Cellular?

Yes. Federal number-portability rules let you keep your number. Keep your current service active, get your account number and transfer PIN from your current carrier, and enter them when you sign up with Consumer Cellular.
