Fine print

Official StraightSAVINGS is $25/month for an eligible new BYOP line with an unlocked, compatible device not active on Straight Talk during the prior 12 months. Carrier states this plan is NOT combinable with an AutoPay discount; a $20 AutoPay price from earlier catalog notes is unsupported and excluded. Online physical SIM starter bundle advertises $29.88 for the first $25 service month plus $4.88 SIM kit, with applicable taxes/fees extra. A separate $50 after-12-month device credit requires additional conditions and is not subtracted.

Frequently asked questions

What network does Straight Talk use?

Straight Talk plans in our catalog run on Verizon’s network. Check the carrier’s coverage map for your address before switching.

What is Straight Talk’s cheapest plan?

The lowest-priced plan we track is StraightSavings Unlimited at $25 a month. That is about $300 over 12 months before any intro deals.

Does Straight Talk have unlimited data plans?

Yes. StraightSavings Unlimited ($25/mo). Check each plan’s high-speed allowance and hotspot rules.

Does Straight Talk offer family or multi-line discounts?

We haven’t found a published multi-line discount on the Straight Talk plans we track, so each line costs the single-line price. Some plans are limited to one line.

Does Straight Talk have phone deals?

We track 2 phone offers from Straight Talk. Most require a specific plan, and many require transferring your number. See the phone deals section for estimated first-year costs.

Can I keep my phone number if I switch to Straight Talk?

Yes. Federal number-portability rules let you keep your number. Keep your current service active, get your account number and transfer PIN from your current carrier, and enter them when you sign up with Straight Talk.
