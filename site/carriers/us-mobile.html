Is US Mobile a good fit?

Good for

  • Budget shoppers: plans from $10/mo.
  • Light users who mostly use Wi-Fi (low-data plans available).
  • Heavy data users (unlimited plans available).
  • People in busy areas who want premium (priority) data.
  • Families: published multi-line pricing.
  • Choosing the network that works best at your address.
  • Hotspot users who need a large allowance.

Watch out for

  • Intro prices end. Budget for the regular price shown here.
  • Standard unlimited data can slow down when towers are busy.
  • Some plans include no hotspot.

Frequently asked questions

What network does US Mobile use?

US Mobile lets you choose among more than one major network when you sign up; the network available depends on your address and phone. Check the carrier’s coverage map for your address before switching.

What is US Mobile’s cheapest plan?

The lowest-priced plan we track is Light 2GB at $10 a month. That is about $120 over 12 months before any intro deals.

Does US Mobile have unlimited data plans?

Yes. Unlimited Starter ($25/mo) and Unlimited Premium ($44/mo). Check each plan’s high-speed allowance and hotspot rules.

Does US Mobile offer family or multi-line discounts?

Yes. For example, Unlimited Starter costs about $100 a month for four lines ($25 per line) at regular prices.

Does US Mobile have phone deals?

We track 1 phone offer from US Mobile. Most require a specific plan, and many require transferring your number. See the phone deals section for estimated first-year costs.

Can I keep my phone number if I switch to US Mobile?

Yes. Federal number-portability rules let you keep your number. Keep your current service active, get your account number and transfer PIN from your current carrier, and enter them when you sign up with US Mobile.
