      Where these deals come from

        How it stays fresh

        Every morning a scheduled job reads these sources and searches the web for new carrier promos. Community posts and carrier-written guides are treated as leads and confirmed with a second source before they’re added. Claude turns what it finds into the deal list, a validator checks the result, and the site republishes. If a run looks wrong (too many deals vanish, bad prices), it’s rejected and yesterday’s list stays up.

        Deals with an end date also watch themselves: once a promo expires, its discount drops out of the math and the deal is marked as ended.
