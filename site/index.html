      Where these deals come from

        How it stays fresh

        See the official-source evidence audit: dated published-price findings, source URLs, checkout questions and the number of offers still awaiting research. If a listing looks wrong, use the reader correction page; a report does not automatically alter any price.

        Every morning, GitHub checks public sources and expires explicitly ended offers. Selected Boost Mobile promotions also receive strict partial checks against official advertised device prices, required plans, and number-transfer wording, with a visible observation history. Missing or conflicting evidence is flagged; no automatic price update or full-deal verification occurs. All other offers still require manual review. No AI or API keys are used.

        Deals with an end date also watch themselves: once a promo expires, its discount drops out of the math; ended phone offers are hidden.

        Editorial maintainers: Open the read-only offer review workbench to investigate unresolved offers and prepare human review notes. Only repository editors can submit signed verifications through GitHub.

        Independent comparisons. Transparent limitations.

        Five Bars is an independent comparison and educational website, not a wireless carrier or checkout service. Prices are estimates from a curated catalog. Most listings have not undergone a recent complete terms review. Always confirm final costs, eligibility, coverage and unlocking directly with the carrier.

        Five Bars currently identifies no paid placements or commission-based rankings. If referral relationships are introduced, we will disclose material compensation clearly near related links and preserve independent rankings.
