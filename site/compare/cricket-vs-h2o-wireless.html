Cricket vs H2O Wireless: AT&T-network plan comparison

Cricket and H2O Wireless are AT&T-network choices in this comparison. Some H2O catalog listings are annual prepaid plans, while Cricket may have phone discounts that require specific service plans. Do not combine a Cricket handset promotion with an unrelated low-cost H2O rate in a savings estimate.

What to compare first

AT&T-network options with different billing rules

  1. An annual prepaid rate represents an effective monthly equivalent, not the amount due each month. Check the full prepaid amount and renewal policy.
  2. Phone promotions may require a minimum number of months of service, a transfer of your number, a specific plan and an unlock waiting period. The device sticker price is not the total cost.
  3. H2O plan-directory pages have blocked the scheduled checker; some price headlines may come only from partial sources. An inaccessible page is neither a confirmed current price nor a confirmed increase.
