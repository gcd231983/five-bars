What to compare first

Verizon-network service with different pricing and plan rules

  1. Visible may advertise temporary discount codes; compare the underlying regular price and confirm expiration, taxes and eligibility on the official page.
  2. Some Total Wireless catalog rates are tied to bring-your-own-phone promotions or multi-line conditions. Verify whether the offer applies to one line before comparing totals.
  3. Hotspot speed limits and priority-data rules can vary even when two plans use the same underlying network. Read each carrier plan disclosure.
