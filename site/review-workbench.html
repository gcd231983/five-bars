Human verification sign-off

When you have personally reviewed a listing’s full source terms and resolved discrepancies, open the protected human review workflow. Choose verify, enter the exact offer ID and carrier’s official public HTTPS URL, then enter the specified attestation phrase. That workflow changes the signed verification ledger after validating inputs. The website does not automatically approve any offers.
