Human verification sign-off

After inspecting an actual carrier checkout or written quote, open the relevant offer worksheet above, check all ten material conditions, enter exact first and renewal payments including taxes and compulsory fees, and copy its anonymized checkout JSON. Open the human review workflow. Choose verify, enter the offer ID, same-provider official public HTTPS evidence URL, paste the JSON into review_details, and type the exact attestation phrase. GitHub records the attributed reviewer and the selected scenario; the website does not automatically approve offers.

Detailed checkout verification instructions. Never enter payment credentials, phone number, transfer PIN, subscriber identity or other private account information in any review field. Missing fee amounts or uncertain enrollment means do not sign off.

A reviewed checkout scenario is NOT a guarantee that a different visitor qualifies, and human review does not make advertised financed-device estimates safe. Recheck complete terms at least every 14 days and whenever prices, financing, prepayment, hotspot, taxes or device-lock conditions change. Full details are in the editorial methodology.
