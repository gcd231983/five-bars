What to know before you switch

  • 5 Mbps handles email, browsing and a standard-quality video call, but it is slow for large downloads.
  • Hotspot use often counts toward your high-speed data allowance.
  • For daily laptop work on cellular, a plan with a large full-speed hotspot allowance is worth paying a little more for.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest plan with hotspot?

In our latest check, Tello Starter 2GB has the lowest estimated 12-month cost at about $120 ($10 a month). Prices change often, so confirm at the carrier before you buy.

How much hotspot data do I need?

Light laptop use (email, documents, browsing) can fit in 5–10GB a month. Video calls use roughly 0.5–1.5GB per hour, and streaming in HD uses more, so remote workers should look for 30GB or more.
