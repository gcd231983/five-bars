Family pricing on prepaid carriers works in two ways. Some carriers lower the per-line price as you add lines; most simply charge the single-line price for each line. We show both, and we never assume a family discount the carrier hasn’t published. Totals marked with ≈ are the single-line price multiplied by the number of lines.

What to know before you switch

  • Compare the total monthly bill for your exact number of lines. The per-line price in ads usually assumes four or more lines.
  • You don’t have to put everyone on the same carrier. A heavy-data user and a light user can each have the plan that fits them.
  • Check whether an intro deal applies to every line or only the first one.
  • Kids and relatives who are mostly on Wi-Fi may do fine on a low-data plan, which costs far less than unlimited.

How we rank

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest family plan for 4 lines?

Among plans with a published multi-line price, Tello Everyday 10GB costs about $60 a month for four lines in our latest check. Carriers without a published family discount charge the single-line price for each line.

Do I need the same plan for every line?

It depends on the carrier. Some let each line pick its own plan; others require one plan for the whole account. If a carrier won’t mix plans, separate accounts, even at different carriers, can still come out cheaper.

Can I keep everyone’s phone numbers when we switch?

Yes. Federal number-portability rules let you move each number to the new carrier. Keep the old service active until every line has transferred.
