What to know before you switch

  • Check coverage for your address on the FCC National Broadband Map and on the carrier’s own map, especially indoors and in rural areas.
  • Most unlocked phones work on T-Mobile’s network, but confirm with the carrier’s IMEI checker.
  • Several T-Mobile-network carriers sell discounted multi-month packages. Compare the renewal price, not just the first term.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest plan on T-Mobile’s network?

In our latest check, Tello Starter 2GB has the lowest estimated 12-month cost at about $120 ($10 a month). Prices change often, so confirm at the carrier before you buy.

Do T-Mobile MVNOs get the same coverage as T-Mobile?

They use T-Mobile’s towers, so coverage is generally the same. Data may be slower during congestion, and some plans limit hotspot or video quality.
