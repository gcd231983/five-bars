What to know before you switch

  • Check coverage for your address on the FCC National Broadband Map and on the carrier’s own map before switching.
  • Confirm your phone is unlocked and supports AT&T’s network bands. Use the carrier’s IMEI checker.
  • Annual plans on AT&T’s network can be very cheap per month, but you pay the whole year upfront.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest plan on AT&T’s network?

In our latest check, H2O Wireless Annual 10GB (pay $192 upfront) has the lowest estimated 12-month cost at about $192, paid upfront for the year. Prices change often, so confirm at the carrier before you buy.

Do AT&T prepaid carriers get the same coverage as AT&T?

They use AT&T’s towers, so coverage is generally the same. Data speeds may be lower when the network is congested.
