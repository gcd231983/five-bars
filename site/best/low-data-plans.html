What to know before you switch

  • Look at your last three months of data use and add a buffer.
  • Check what happens when you run out. Some plans slow you down; others stop data until you add more.
  • Turn on Wi-Fi calling and set apps to update only on Wi-Fi to stretch a small plan.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest low-data plan?

In our latest check, Good2Go Mobile Annual 1GB (pay $60 upfront) has the lowest estimated 12-month cost at about $60, paid upfront for the year. Prices change often, so confirm at the carrier before you buy.

Is 5GB of data enough?

For many people who are on Wi-Fi at home and at work, yes: 5GB covers maps, messaging, email and some music. Streaming video over cellular uses roughly 0.7–1GB per hour in standard quality, so heavy video users should size up.
