What to know before you switch

  • The phone is usually locked to the carrier for a set period, often 60 days to 12 months.
  • The required plan is often a pricier unlimited tier. Compare against buying an unlocked phone and choosing a cheaper plan.
  • Online, in-store and retailer prices can differ, and activation fees may apply.

How we rank

  • We add the advertised device price to the plan you are required to buy, then count 12 months of that plan at its regular price.
  • Mail-in rebates, gift cards and bill credits are left out because they are not cash at checkout.
  • Offers that are out of stock, on hold or paid as postpaid bill credits are listed separately, not ranked.
  • Taxes and activation fees are not included unless the carrier publishes them.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest free phone deal right now?

In our latest check, the Samsung Galaxy A17 5G from Total Wireless costs about $425 in the first year, including the required $35/mo plan. Confirm the price and eligibility at the carrier before buying.

Are free phones really free?

The device price can be $0, but you must buy service to get it, usually on a specific plan and often with a number transfer. The real cost is the plan you’re required to keep, which is why we rank by first-year cost.
