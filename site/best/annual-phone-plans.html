What to know before you switch

  • If the carrier sells a monthly version, try it for a month first to make sure coverage works for you.
  • Set a reminder before renewal. The renewal price and terms may change.
  • Make sure your phone is unlocked and works on the carrier’s network before you commit for a year.

How we rank

  • We estimate what one line costs over 12 months at the regular price. AutoPay-style discounts that anyone can get are included; intro deals that need a new line or number transfer are shown separately.
  • Annual plans count their full upfront payment.
  • Offers whose published price conflicts with our records are held for review and not ranked.
  • No carrier pays for placement. Affiliate links, where they exist, never change the order.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest annual phone plan?

In our latest check, Good2Go Mobile Annual 1GB (pay $60 upfront) has the lowest estimated 12-month cost at about $60, paid upfront for the year. Prices change often, so confirm at the carrier before you buy.

Can I get a refund on an annual phone plan?

Usually not. Most annual prepaid plans are non-refundable once activated. Check the carrier’s terms before buying.
