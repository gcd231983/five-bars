What to know before you switch

  • Look for the high-speed allowance in the plan name or fine print (for example “30GB high-speed”). Past that point, speeds drop sharply for the rest of the month.
  • Premium (priority) data matters if you live or work somewhere crowded, like a downtown, stadium or campus. Elsewhere, standard data is usually fine.
  • Check hotspot rules separately. Some unlimited plans include no hotspot at all; others limit it to 5 Mbps.
  • Cheaper unlimited plans usually stream video in standard definition (about 480p) to save data.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest unlimited plan?

In our latest check, MobileX Unlimited 10 (10GB high-speed) has the lowest estimated 12-month cost at about $178.56 ($14.88 a month). Prices change often, so confirm at the carrier before you buy.

Is unlimited data really unlimited?

You won’t be cut off or charged overage, but most prepaid unlimited plans have a high-speed allowance or lower priority. After the allowance, speeds can drop to levels that are fine for messages and email but slow for video.

What is deprioritization?

When a cell tower is congested, the carrier serves its premium customers first. Deprioritized data keeps working but may be slower until the congestion clears. Plans with premium data are exempt, usually up to a monthly limit.
