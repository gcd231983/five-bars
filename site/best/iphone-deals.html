What to know before you switch

  • Check whether the deal requires a number transfer, a new line or a specific unlimited plan.
  • Carrier-discounted iPhones are usually locked for a period. If you travel abroad or plan to switch soon, an unlocked iPhone may be worth the higher upfront price.
  • Postpaid “free iPhone” deals are paid out as monthly bill credits over two or three years. They are not comparable to prepaid prices, so we list them separately.

How we rank

  • We add the advertised device price to the plan you are required to buy, then count 12 months of that plan at its regular price.
  • Mail-in rebates, gift cards and bill credits are left out because they are not cash at checkout.
  • Offers that are out of stock, on hold or paid as postpaid bill credits are listed separately, not ranked.
  • Taxes and activation fees are not included unless the carrier publishes them.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest prepaid iPhone deal right now?

In our latest check, the iPhone 16e from Metro by T-Mobile costs about $704.99 in the first year, including the required $50/mo plan. Confirm the price and eligibility at the carrier before buying.

Is it cheaper to buy an iPhone from a prepaid carrier?

Often, if you were going to switch anyway and the required plan fits your needs. If the required plan costs more than you would otherwise pay, an unlocked iPhone and a cheaper plan can come out ahead within a year.
