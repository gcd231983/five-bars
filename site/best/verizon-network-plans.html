What to know before you switch

  • Check coverage for your address on the FCC National Broadband Map and on the carrier’s own map before switching.
  • Make sure your phone is unlocked and compatible. Every carrier has an IMEI checker on its website.
  • Prepaid lines can get lower priority than Verizon’s premium postpaid customers when towers are busy, unless the plan includes premium data.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest plan on Verizon’s network?

In our latest check, MobileX Unlimited 10 (10GB high-speed) has the lowest estimated 12-month cost at about $178.56 ($14.88 a month). Prices change often, so confirm at the carrier before you buy.

Do Verizon MVNOs get the same coverage as Verizon?

They use the same towers, so the coverage map is generally the same. Speeds can differ when the network is congested, and some features (like certain roaming or 5G bands) can vary by plan.
