## Questions to resolve for any partner before adding links

1. Eligibility: New publisher site allowed? Any required monthly visitors, geography, tax/business structure, content rules or exclusivity?
2. Referral terms: Qualifying plans, exclusions, validated activation window, cancellation chargebacks, payout schedule and refund handling.
3. Marketing policy: SEO comparison sites permitted? Merchant trademarks, screenshots, paid search, email campaigns, discount codes and sub-affiliates restricted?
4. Measurement: Network consent requirements, cookies, tracking pixels and potential impact on privacy disclosures. Avoid adding tracking until reviewed.
5. Price integrity: Can the carrier supply accurate data feeds, broadband labels and official current-term URLs? What is the correction contact and update cadence?
6. Commercial disclosure: How should a nearby clearly visible material-connection statement be phrased? Are price comparisons independent and unaltered?
7. Link implementation: Use only carrier/network-approved destination and campaign URLs after acceptance; store partner metadata separately from independent product rankings.

## Actual workflow and stop conditions

1. **Before outreach:** have a small set of fully checked offers, a tested calculator, a private contact channel and honest public editorial/legal pages. Keep documentation of tests.
2. **Initial application:** submit only true traffic/business facts and the existing Five Bars links. Never claim current relationships or user testimonials without consent.
3. **After acceptance:** record agreement version, eligible carriers/plans, approved target URLs, commission structure, restrictions, terms update dates, conversion privacy review and cancellation handling.
4. **Before publishing a paid link:** require independent legal/disclosure review, adjacent material-connection notice and tests that ranking order is unaffected by compensation.
5. **Audit continually:** if an offer loses verified terms or its pricing conflicts with an official source, withhold or de-emphasize the unsupported cost claim even when affiliate payout might be available.

**Not a promise of revenue.** Even a public application page is only an invitation to apply. Business registration, tax setup, program approval and signed terms require the actual owner's involvement.


## 4.0 correction process and link-disclosure requirements

A visitor can report a suspected listing error through a local-only form and GitHub public issue template. **Never** let an affiliate payout override a documented conflict or treat user-submitted text as independently verified pricing. [Corrections policy](CORRECTIONS_POLICY.md) specifies human triage and regression requirements.

If an affiliate contract is actually signed later, place a clear, plain-language **commission disclosure immediately adjacent to each compensated link or recommendation**, not only in the website footer or disclosures policy. Follow the [FTC Endorsement Guides FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking) and seek appropriate legal/compliance review. Do not mark presently uncompensated links as affiliate links. No links, pixels or commissions are enabled by 4.0.
